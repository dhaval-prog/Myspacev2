Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

MySpace ("we", "our", "the app") helps you keep track of your rooms and belongings, personal and shared budgets, and shared expenses with other people. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and what control you have over it.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Your name and email address, and a profile photo if you choose to add one.
  • If you sign in with Google, Facebook, or Apple, the basic profile information those providers share with us (typically your name, email address, and profile photo) — we never see or store your password for those accounts.

As you use the app, we store the data you enter, including:

  • Rooms and items you add, including names, categories, and expiry dates.
  • Budget cards and expenses you log, and any budget cards you join.
  • Split groups you create or join, along with the expenses, settlements, and chat messages within them.

How we use your information

We use your information only to operate the app: to show you your own data, to share the relevant parts of it with people you've explicitly invited into a budget card or split group, and to keep your account secure. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Sharing with other people

Information is only visible to other people when you choose to share it — by inviting someone to a budget card or a split group. Members of a shared space can see the activity within that space (its expenses, members, and chat), but never your other, unrelated data.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored using Supabase, a hosted database and authentication provider, with access controls that ensure only you (and anyone you've explicitly shared a space with) can read it.

Your choices and rights

From Account Settings → Data & Privacy inside the app, you can:

  • Download a copy of your data.
  • Export your inventory, budgets, and expense history individually.
  • Control who can see your profile within shared spaces.

From Account Settings → Danger Zone, you can permanently delete all of your data while keeping your account, or delete your account entirely — both actions take effect immediately and cannot be undone.

Children's privacy

MySpace is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
