How to delete your data

Last updated: 28 August 2026

This page explains how to delete your information from MySpace, including data associated with signing in via Google, Facebook, or Apple.

Delete your data from inside the app

  1. Open MySpace and sign in.
  2. Go to Account Settings → Danger Zone.
  3. Choose Delete my data to erase your rooms, items, budgets, and expense history while keeping your account, or Delete my account to permanently remove your account and all associated data.

Both actions take effect immediately and cannot be undone.

Request deletion by email

If you signed in with Facebook, Google, or Apple and can't access the app, you can request deletion directly by emailing damaniadhaval.dd@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. We'll confirm your identity and permanently delete your account and data within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account removes your profile information, rooms and items, budget cards and expenses you own, and split groups you own, along with your authentication record. Shared budget cards or split groups you were only a member of (not the owner) are not deleted, since other members' data depends on them — you're simply removed as a member.
